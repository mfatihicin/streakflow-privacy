3. Yedekleme, dışa aktarma ve silme

  • Android otomatik yedek: Cihazında Google yedeklemesi açıksa Android, StreakFlow'un alışkanlık verilerini ve tercihlerini kendi Google hesabındaki yedeğe ekler; yeni telefona geçince otomatik geri gelir. İlerleme fotoğrafları bulut yedeğine dahil edilmez (yalnız telefondan telefona doğrudan aktarımda taşınır). Bu yedek Google'ın hizmetidir; biz yedeğe erişemeyiz. Kapatmak için: Android Ayarlar › Google › Yedekleme.
  • iPhone yedeği: iCloud yedeklemesi veya bilgisayar yedeği açıksa, iOS uygulama verilerini (fotoğraflar dahil) kendi Apple hesabındaki ya da bilgisayarındaki yedeğe ekleyebilir. Bu Apple'ın hizmetidir; biz erişemeyiz.
  • Elle yedek ve CSV (Premium): "Verileri Yedekle" ve "CSV olarak dışa aktar" bir dosya oluşturur ve paylaşım ekranını açar. Dosya yalnız senin seçtiğin yere (Dosyalar, Drive, e-posta vb.) gider.
  • Silme: Ayarlar › Tüm verileri sil ile cihazdaki verileri kalıcı olarak silebilirsin. Uygulamayı kaldırmak da cihazdaki verileri siler. Bulut yedeklerini Google / Apple hesabının ayarlarından silebilirsin.

  • iPhone: İzin verirsen adım sayın Apple Sağlık'tan okunur (adım hedefli alışkanlıklar otomatik ilerlesin diye) ve su alışkanlığında girdiğin miktar Apple Sağlık'a su (DietaryWater) olarak yazılır.

  • Adım ve su verileri sağlık verisi niteliğinde olabilir. Bu veriler yalnız cihazında işlenir; bize, reklam ortaklarına veya başka bir üçüncü kişiye hiçbir zaman gönderilmez, reklam, pazarlama veya profil çıkarma için kullanılmaz, satılmaz.
  • İzinleri istediğin an geri alabilirsin: iPhone'da Sağlık uygulaması › Paylaşım › Uygulamalar › StreakFlow; Android'de Health Connect ayarları.

In short

  • No account and no server of our own. Your habits, streaks, mood entries and photos are stored on your device; we cannot see them.
  • On Android, if the system's automatic backup is on, your habit data (not photos) is included in the backup in your own Google account; on iPhone, your device backup (iCloud or computer) may include app data.
  • Health data (steps, water) is exchanged with Apple Health / Health Connect only with your permission and only on your device; it never reaches us or ad partners.
  • The free version shows ads. Ad partners (Unity LevelPlay and the networks it mediates) process device data, abroad.
  • Purchases are made through Apple / Google and verified with RevenueCat.

2. Data that stays on your device

The following is stored on your phone (and on your watch, if paired). It is not sent to us, so we do not process or see it:

  • your habits, goals, completion records, streaks, XP, badges and vacation days;
  • your "How are you today?" mood entries and notes;
  • your progress photos (only if you add them from the camera or gallery; kept in the app's own folder and never uploaded to any server);
  • your reminders (local notifications scheduled on the device);
  • theme, language and other preferences; home-screen widget data.

Smartwatch: if you use the Apple Watch or Wear OS app, today's habits and progress are sent from your phone to your paired watch through the operating system's device-to-device channel. This data does not reach us.

Siri and Google Assistant: if you complete a habit by voice, your speech is processed by Apple's or Google's own assistant service under their privacy policies. StreakFlow does not record audio; the command passed by the assistant is handled on your device.

3. Backup, export and deletion

  • Android Auto Backup: if Google backup is on, Android adds StreakFlow's habit data and preferences to the backup in your own Google account, restored automatically on a new phone. Progress photos are not included in the cloud backup (they move only in a direct phone-to-phone transfer). This backup is a Google service; we cannot access it. To turn it off: Android Settings › Google › Backup.
  • iPhone backup: if iCloud Backup or a computer backup is on, iOS may include app data (including photos) in the backup in your Apple account or on your computer. This is an Apple service; we cannot access it.
  • Manual backup and CSV (Premium): "Backup Data" and "Export as CSV" create a file and open the share sheet. The file goes only where you choose (Files, Drive, e-mail, etc.).
  • Deletion: Settings › Delete all data permanently deletes the data on the device. Uninstalling the app also deletes it. You can delete cloud backups in your Google / Apple account settings.

4. Health data (Apple Health / Health Connect)

  • iPhone: with your permission, your step count is read from Apple Health (so step habits progress automatically), and the amount you log in a water habit is written to Apple Health as water (DietaryWater).
  • Android: with your permission, your step count is read from Health Connect. The app may also try to write water entries to Health Connect; this happens only if you grant that permission.
  • Step and water data may count as health data. It is processed only on your device; it is never sent to us, to ad partners or to any other third party, is never used for advertising, marketing or profiling, and is never sold.
  • You can revoke these permissions at any time: on iPhone in the Health app › Sharing › Apps › StreakFlow; on Android in Health Connect settings.

9. International transfers

Our service providers' servers are outside Türkiye, so transferring the data listed above to countries such as the USA, Israel, the Russian Federation, the UAE and Singapore is technically necessary for the app to work. The Turkish Personal Data Protection Board has not issued an adequacy decision for these countries. These transfers fall under Article 9 of Law No. 6698 (KVKK). As of today, no KVKK standard contract has been signed with these providers and notified to the Authority; for these transfers we rely on the data-protection commitments in the providers' own terms (data processing agreements and the technical and organisational security measures based on them). We are asking the providers to sign the standard contract published by the Board under Article 9; if one is signed, it will be notified to the Authority within 5 business days as the law requires, and this notice will be updated. We try to keep the transferred data to the minimum the service needs.

For users in the EU/EEA, transfers rely on the safeguards offered by the providers, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
